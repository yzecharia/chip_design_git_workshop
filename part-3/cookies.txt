Quick Chocolate Chip Cookies — makes ~24

Ingredients

200 g butter, softened
100 g brown sugar
75 g white sugar
2 large eggs
2 tsp vanilla extract
320 g flour
1 tsp baking soda
½ tsp salt
300 g chocolate chips

Directions

Heat the oven to 180°C and line two trays with baking paper.
Beat the butter and both sugars until light and fluffy.
Mix in the eggs and vanilla until smooth.
Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt just until no dry flour remains.
Fold in the chocolate chips.
Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the trays, spaced well apart.
Bake 10–12 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centres still look soft.
Cool on the tray 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

Pull them while the centres look underdone — they finish setting on the hot tray.
